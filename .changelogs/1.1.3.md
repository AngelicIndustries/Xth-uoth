* Added the missing Aqua, Praecantatio, and Permutatio aspects to "Combining Potions", fixing research note creation and instant unlocking with Salis Arcana ([#1](https://github.com/JackOfNoneTrades/Xth-uoth/issues/1)).
* Added a config toggle for Prysmal ore generation, which remains enabled by default ([#2](https://github.com/JackOfNoneTrades/Xth-uoth/issues/2)).
* Fixed the Immixer reusing its last emulsifier indefinitely and incorrectly splitting inventory stacks ([#3](https://github.com/JackOfNoneTrades/Xth-uoth/issues/3)).
* Corrected the Aberrant biome's terrain blocks and gave it a custom `ABERRANT` biome type, stopped Xth'uoth from changing the shared Overworld spawn-biome list, and blacklisted its dimension from Ars Magica 2 world generation ([#4](https://github.com/JackOfNoneTrades/Xth-uoth/issues/4)).
* Rifts now use the mod's animated portal texture instead of the static oval, with transparency and support for resource-pack reloads ([#5](https://github.com/JackOfNoneTrades/Xth-uoth/issues/5)).
* Rifts now use vanilla portal charge-up and distortion, require stepping inside to travel, and keep the return cooldown active until you step out. Removed the added Nausea potion effect and stopped rifts from spreading taint on the client.
* Fixed a client biome lookup crash when returning to the Overworld before its chunks finish loading.
