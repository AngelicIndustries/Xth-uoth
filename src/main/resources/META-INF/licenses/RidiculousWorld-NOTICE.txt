The mob is renamed and restricted to natural spawning in Xth'uoth, and uses the
native aberration creature attribute. Its renderer uses Minecraft's slime models,
as does the upstream renderer. The shader loader is implemented for Xth'uoth;
RidiculousWorld's separately licensed ShaderHelper is not included.
